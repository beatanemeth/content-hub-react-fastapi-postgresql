| **Revenue** | Coffee bean sales, café, paid courses                                                                                                                   |

Bloom & Burr roasts and serves speciality coffee and teaches people to brew it. It makes speciality coffee accessible without jargon or price barriers. It brings home brewers, small roasters, baristas and café owners together around shared tasting and learning. Its guiding idea comes from the late roaster Hannah Ruth: good coffee is a conversation between farmer, roaster, barista and guest, and that conversation should stay kind.

**Who they serve:**

- Beginners and home brewers at any age
- Baristas and café owners
- Small independent roasters
- Partners and families of coffee enthusiasts
